# Corps et bras illustrés — version 3

Outil : ImageGen intégré, mode génération avec référence visuelle `portraits-v2.png`, fond transparent.

Les atlas `bodies-v3.png` et `arms-v3.png` sont assemblés et articulés par `js/monster-body-art.js`. Les sources intégrées permettent également l’ouverture locale du jeu sans serveur. Les images originales générées sont conservées dans le dossier de génération Codex.

## Prompt des corps

Use case: stylized-concept. ONE game sprite atlas, exactly 12 separate components in a regular 4 columns by 3 rows grid, equal square cells on genuinely transparent background. Generous transparent gutters; each component wholly inside its cell, no overlaps. No text, no labels, no grids, no scenery, no ground shadows, no weapons, no balls. Production 2D paper-doll game assets. Match the materials, palette and detailed soft-shaded digital illustration of the 12 monster head portraits in the reference image. Charming polished family-friendly fantasy RPG look: convincing fabric folds, dimensional armor, leather stitching, layered fur/feathers, glossy highlights, warm shadows; NOT flat geometric vector art. Front view, consistent lighting from upper left.
Exact row-major species:
row1 goblin (olive green, brown stitched leather and brass), teal scaled lizard-man (amber scales, tan leather), lavender witch (rich purple cloth, embroidered gold edges), ivory skeleton warrior (weathered steel and bronze);
row2 moss-green orc berserker (dark leather, weathered iron, ivory spikes), pink-lilac harpy (layered lilac feathers and gold details), brown-gray wolf beast-man (fur and rugged brown leather), lime-green venom slime (translucent jelly, bubbles and bright reflections);
row3 violet shadow specter (tattered deep-purple ethereal cloth), blue-gray cave troll (rough stone skin, moss, heavy worn leather), ochre spotted hyena gnoll (spotted fur, dark leather wraps), golden-brown mandrake (root bark, vines, green leaves).
Asset: HEADLESS AND ARMLESS BODY atlas. Each cell contains exactly one complete compact chibi body from neckline/shoulders down to both feet. NO head, face, eyes, hair, hat, arms or hands. Shoulder armor belongs to torso but no arm below. Rounded expressive proportions compatible with large detailed heads: broad short torso and short legs, no realistic tall human proportions. Both legs visible, feet level, relaxed standing front-view stance. Detailed belts, buckles, straps, shoulder pads, fur tufts, seams and boots suited to species. Harpy has small feathered body and bird feet, specter a tapered floating tattered robe with no legs, mandrake bark torso and two root feet. Slime is one rounded faceless gelatin body without legs. Everything self contained, no disjoint decorations. Keep neckline centered horizontally near top of component and feet near bottom. Transparent neck opening is allowed, no black neck stump.

Isolated limb from rounded shoulder at top down through upper arm and elbow to wrist and hand at bottom. NO torso, head, body, second arm, feet, props or detached pieces. Arm hangs vertically almost straight with only a slight natural bend, so we can articulate it at the elbow in a game engine. Elbow halfway down, wrist at about 80 percent of total length. Entire hand visible, fingers grouped in a softly rounded mitten-like shape, recognizable thumb protrudes toward the LEFT of the image, toward the absent torso; never a fist blob or a mitten cuff seam across the skin. Premium shaded, detailed rendering matching the reference head art. Arms substantial enough to show material detail at small game scale. Upper and forearms nearly equal length, hand compact but clearly articulated.
